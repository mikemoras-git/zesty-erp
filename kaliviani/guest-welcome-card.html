A note on these books

During the restoration of the complex, the late owner's handwritten books came to light — left in the drawer of a table, kept there intact for decades until the day we opened it.

We felt it our duty to share them with the guests of this house, and made this archive to accompany you through your stay.

It remains a work in progress — we go on transcribing, translating, and enriching the manuscripts we found.
