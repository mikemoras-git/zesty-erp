ETF PORTFOLIO - TEST IT ON YOUR LAPTOP (before putting it online)
==================================================================

This folder runs the app on your own computer only. Nothing is uploaded.
Live prices work exactly as they will on Papaki.

WINDOWS
  Double-click  Start-Windows.bat
  If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
  "More info" > "Run anyway" (only the first time).

MAC
  Double-click  Start-Mac.command
  The first time, macOS may block it: right-click it > Open > Open.
  If it asks to install developer tools / Python, accept, then try again.

Your browser opens at  http://localhost:8765
A black window stays open while the app runs. Close it to stop.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Data is saved in this browser on this laptop only.
- Sync between laptops works only in the online version.
- To try the app with sample data, click "Load demo data" on the
  Dashboard. Delete it later in Settings > Delete all data.
- Keeping your test data when you go online:
  Settings > Export backup here, then Import backup in the online version.
- Do NOT upload this "local" folder to Papaki. It isn't needed there
  (and it's blocked by .htaccess in case it gets uploaded).
